### Agile Software Development

1. Describe how your team plans, prioritizes, and schedules work.
2. Describe the methodology is used to estimate work.
3. Can you describe how much work - equivalent to the number of sprints - is present on your backlog or to-do list?
4. Can you describe how many products your team is currently working on simultaneously?
5. How do members of the team collaborate?
6. How does the team gather and measure feedback from customers and users?
7. How do coordinate or split work up across teams?
8. Describe how your team collaborates, shares risks, innovates, and learns.
9. Describe how your team handles risk with a project.
10. Describe how your team regularly comes together to share and evaluate new ideas.

### Continuous Security

1. Describe when your team conducts security testing.
2. How extensively is security testing conducted, and what is your team's approach to incident response in the event of a breach?
3. What code scanning tools are utilized in the continuous integration (CI) workflow?
4. How does your team make sure that there is enough of a focus on security while writing code?
5. How frequently does your team conduct security assessments on the code?
6. How does your team handle the management of passwords and other credentials?
7. Explain the approach your team takes in conducting threat analysis.
8. What factors related to compliance and governance are taken into account when building apps?

### Culture

1. Which of these DevOps practices are commonplace in your organization or team?

- Our change management process involves stakeholders from all aspects of the software development lifecycle.
- Stakeholders in different roles working on the same product communicate frequently and efficiently.
- The organization operates platforms and services for cross functional teams to consume.When we build a workload or application we understand that any new product we create will be a service that needs long-term maintenance and investment.
- We seek a diversity of views when it comes to IT delivery
- Our mission statement is aligned across all teams that participate in the software development lifecycle. _This also includes traditional "business"-functions._
